Privacy Model & Data Control

ShieldLens operates strictly under a local-first, zero-telemetry architecture.

🔒 Privacy Principles

  • Local Analysis Only: 100% of link hover analysis, file type verification, and intent tracking is executed locally on your device inside the extension Service Worker.
  • Zero Remote Transmission: No browsing URLs, history, download metadata, or personal data are ever sent to remote servers.
  • Zero Sensitive Data Capture: Form inputs, passwords, messages, and personal information are strictly ignored.
  • Transient Intent Memory: User click gesture contexts are stored temporarily in volatile memory and automatically discarded after 5-10 seconds.
